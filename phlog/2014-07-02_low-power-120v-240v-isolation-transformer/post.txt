Low Power 120V+240V Isolation Transformer
=========================================

Since I was tired of using the flash circuit hack [1], or my version of it [2],
to power my tube experiments I finally decided to build a isolation transformer
using two identical step-down transformers as suggested by Mike in his nixie
clock documentation [3]. Here are some photos of it:

View of the inside with the transformers back-to-back:
gopher://nathanpc.eu/I/phlog/2014-07-02_low-power-120v-240v-isolation-transformer/inside.jpg

Outside of the case with banana jacks and labels:
gopher://nathanpc.eu/I/phlog/2014-07-02_low-power-120v-240v-isolation-transformer/case.jpg

I'm planing to build a adjustable HV lab power supply in the future (which I may
sell as a kit), so stay tuned.
